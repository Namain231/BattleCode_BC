## 2. Risks requiring concrete acceptance checks

CPU is the immediate blocking engineering risk. cpu_audit.md measured 76,876,136 complete-turn points, only 23.12M below the 100M kill threshold; reaching the repo's 65M target needs 11.88M savings. F1 still peaks at 74.72M. Those are sampled maxima, not bounds; Schooltime normally disables the net, so disabling inference does not fix this peak. Parsing/init and output lie partly outside think's clock. Apply exact stamped reach membership first, verify identical reach labels/order, then measure the combined artifact across both seats, additional seeds and long/high-unit trajectories. Budget all optional evaluations cumulatively with a valid fallback; interrupted survival searches must never certify safety. Measure new input building, round-zero self-test and any continuation call separately. Do not add an assumed 18M call to an already 77M trajectory and declare it safe.

Mandatory promotion is >=22/40 versus baselines/y7_12_nn1 and >=10/20 versus V7.5 on all ten live maps/both seats, followed by the decisive ladder panel (AGENTS.md). The yardstick's 50% parity gate is different. Also regress versus the frozen current base to isolate component changes. Pair queen deaths by map/seed/seat and identify queens from team plus id<=1, not MY_TEAM. Early trapped deaths need explicit expectations; a blanket min-round check must distinguish genuine new early deaths from both controls dying at r5. Report invalid-action/feed deaths separately from timeout failures.

Queen-death modes need confirmed-dead versus suspected-dead versus alive state, freshness and refutation; silence cannot elect a keeper. Fixed official queens never acquire successors. Both dead and equal living queen lengths can reach lower tiebreaks. E2 needs competing/stale keeper claims, simultaneous death, child initialization, unit-floor and elimination tests; E1 needs paid-step legality, portals, changing tails and freshly confirmed occupancy. Do not send our queen on a kamikaze ram. The 118/142 statistic is conditional on top wins reaching r500, not all games, and is not evidence E2 improves us (queen_succession_and_hunt.md).

Sealed sonar changes behavior as well as authenticity: disabling unsigned elections, altered ray allocation, opening guards, shorter expiry and 64-word processing cap may reduce useful reports. Check priority ordering under capped inboxes, same-round versus delayed delivery, old-report resealing, self-play/foreign-key rejection, unknown-map tails, and true-queen feed/avoidance counts against control. Keys must stay out of public diffs/artifacts; plaintext sender IDs and traffic still reveal queen identity. Metadata redesign is another candidate change, not a free hardening patch (AGENT_LOG b19_f1_round2; sonar_echo_tactics.md; f1_h2h.md).

Same-time ladder control needs an actual reserved schedule and immutable submitted sources, matching opponents/maps and interleaved seats/arms. Compare against the real champion as well as the current base where needed; vB19-only A/B cannot establish champion promotion. At 60 games/hour, 100 games per arm consumes at least four quota windows before retries/opponent limits. Autoscrims and activation restoration need director ownership; do not wait until the freeze day to discover quota/tool problems.

Disk is still a live blocker despite cleanup. ~2.89GB must cover shards, exports/checkpoints, temporary uploads, replays and WSL VHD growth. A nominal 6GB cap does not create 6GB free. Check actual space at each stage, retain compact provenance/pair metrics, decode without pickle caches, and delete a source replay only after its shard and manifest are durably written. Use <=4 data workers, RAM guards, and <=6 game workers with other work running (AGENTS.md; dataset_v6 section 7).

## 3. Search: bounded research only

S0 is negative prioritization evidence, not an exhaustive ceiling: prior-visible killer heads miss self/wall traps and information gathering; legal alternatives do not prove survival. Local 16.79% versus top 16.74% supplies no relative advantage. S1 establishes four-game exogenous-spawn oracle parity, not imagined-world fidelity, seeded current-engine parity, wasm cost or strength (search_s0_result.md; engine_sim_status.md).

Respect the authorized S1b/S3 track, but give it a separate owner and a hard 10-04 stop; do not divert trainer/CPU/gate capacity. S3 currently cannot start because the installed judge rejects --serve and rebuild prerequisites are missing. Avoid an open-ended toolchain rebuild merely to get a research number. Team-wide served observation memory also gives an advantage unavailable to the shipped per-dragon bot; restrict it to per-dragon legal memory to attribute gains to search.

Cheapest next discriminating test: use the existing Python simulator on a small stratified set of saved visible tactical decisions (queens, head-on, wall/self traps), compare the unchanged chooser with depth-3 candidate search under identical legal observations and a fixed opponent policy, and report action changes, survival/material/queen outcomes, and sensitivity to opponent responses. Reuse exogenous spawns only as a labeled hindsight upper bound. This can falsify tactical value without C++/MLP/toolchain work; positive results do not prove ladder gain. If the served harness is already cheaply recoverable, the stronger direct test is paired net-only versus search with the same harness, maps/seeds/seats and memory, as search_s3_result.md proposes. A 20-game arm cannot resolve a modest gain; require a large repeatable mechanism effect before spending more. No submission search before qualifiers.
