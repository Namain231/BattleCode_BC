# S4 handoff summary (1 Oct 2026)

## Line and status
- S4.7 is the Abyss-framework rewrite, split into small headers: `board.hpp` (torus topology, edges/portals, beds); `common.hpp` (shared types, Params, timing); `gossip.hpp` (authenticated sonar messages); `knownmap.hpp` (map matching and map-derived Params); `nav.hpp` (BFS/flood and time-aware occupancy); `policy.hpp` (roles, targets, scoring, splits, feeding); `world.hpp` (per-process observations, pearl belief, body and crowd memory). `io.hpp` handles protocol parsing/output; `main.cpp` wires the turn loop.
- Best retained reference is `baselines/S4.7.5` / `viper_vS4.7`: 50.0% on its 6-map, 3-seed, both-side panel vs `viper_v7.12`; S4.7.6 scored 8-22 (27%) in 30 real ladder games vs three 1700+ teams. It is not the active champion.
- Later S4.24 (arrival-time pearl belief) scored 62% on a 32-game panel, but was 54.4% after 125 SPRT games, undecided and shelved. S4.26's corrected time-aware other-body search scored 34% vs the baseline's 53% panel result. No later build supersedes S4.7.5 as a validated reference.

## Findings and comparison with our line
- Early replay rates conflict with our raw-intake finding: S4's 184 top-team replays put our 0.104 eats/dragon-turn inside the top-16 range (0.066-0.119; median ~0.09), with similar net growth (+12.7 vs +12.4 per 1,000 dragon-turns). Our LAB/AGENTS report top teams eat about 2.3x our total maze pearls in r0-100. These use different denominators/samples; reconcile by map, rounds, and total dragon-turns before choosing “early economy” or “late economy” as the lever.
- S4 sees a late slowdown: our r300+ eating 0.057/dragon-turn, 56% adjacent-pearl greed, 10.2 splits/1,000 turns; top teams usually keep eating/splitting later. S4's feed channel recycled 69% of its pearls to allies and left 24% uneaten, vs sampled top channels recycling 85-95%. This agrees with our measured weak delivery, but later schedule/feed-distance changes failed to beat a same-time control; mechanism gains did not establish a win gain.
- S4's “top early game” conclusion does not mean equal total opening growth: per-dragon-turn rates can match while population/dragon-turns and raw intake differ. Our Trophy traces show a specific contested-room access failure; test that by map and round rather than averaging across maps.
- Slithery/Schooltime were omitted from S4's initial local panel; real games exposed severe losses. Corrected diagnosis: Slithery's high wall/self death counts are not by themselves a bug (both sides can exceed 600-700); S4 losses were already behind about 28v42 units at r50. Schooltime had 265/574 head-to-head deaths in three games and remains a distinct, incompletely traced failure.
- “Round 50 decides” is S4-vs-v7.12-specific, not a universal claim: the top-16 replay profile found our champion's early rates in-band, while our own ladder data still shows a large opening pearl-total gap. Keep both hypotheses live with matched per-map measurements.
- Zig judge: S4 reports parity with sandbox on outcome, rounds, turns, and counters, including identical replay results; a Trophy run took 58.9s vs 142.7s (~2.4x faster). Use it for faster local batches, but keep the backend fixed within every comparison.

## Portable ideas for `viper_nn`
- `knownmap.hpp::KnownMapMatcher::adapt()`: consider structure-derived tuning (bed/wall density) instead of more map-name gates. Expected: smoother known-map behavior. Do not use raw local self-sensed density as a global estimate; that fallback regressed maps_gen. Test one parameter at a time on official and generated maps with the same judge.
- `world.hpp::World::crowd` + `policy.hpp::Policy::scoreFrom()`: a small decaying teammate-traffic penalty could reduce repeated corridor convergence. This is an ablation, not a proven gain; preserve hard safety/role logic and measure head-on rate, early intake, and r50 population edge.
- `policy.hpp::buildTargets()` / arrival-time belief in S4.24: audit semantics, but do not duplicate blindly—`viper_nn/main.cpp::pearlProb()` already evaluates at `ROUND + distance`. Only port a demonstrated difference; compare action choices and pearl intake on identical replays first.
- `nav.hpp`'s time-aware other-dragon body freeing is not portable as-is: fixed S4.26 still fell to 34% vs 53%, plausibly because permeable teammate bodies remove spatial separation. Avoid transplanting without coordination and a same-replay correctness check.

## Tools to reuse
- Prefer `tools/bceval_batch.py` plus `battlecode-eval` for dominant/early/late-collapse/recovery labels; use as a complement to `game_review.py`/`loss_digest.py`, not as a death-cause oracle (66-84% win-probability accuracy).
- Use `tools/tempo.py` for population edge at r25/50/100/150 and `tools/concentration.py` only among games reaching the length tiebreak; these are clearer mechanism metrics than aggregate win rate. `growth_pattern.py` adds winner/loser curves; `death_context.py` adds context around deaths beyond raw counts.
- Keep our paired/interleaved `top_scrims.py` and per-game replay review. S4's standard is useful: all live maps, both sides, fixed Zig judge, saved replays, mechanism metrics before the score; inspect raw timeout/error rows.
