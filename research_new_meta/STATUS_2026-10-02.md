## Executive summary

Qualifiers are 2026-10-09 on the ten live maps; candidate freeze is planned around 10-07 (task context). The working foundation is baselines/vB19, with viper_b19_f1 sealed sonar and viper_b19_v4 learnGap as candidate components. No reviewed report proves the combined foundation beats the real champion under the new rules. Frozen baselines retain old-rule pedigree only (AGENTS.md banner; insights.md banner).

Proven within limited tests: F1b authenticates telemetry and preserves observation age; native rejection tests and replay delivery checks pass (AGENT_LOG.md, b19_f1_round2). The stale network still appears useful: drone wNN=2 scored 23/40 versus 11/40 for wNN=0 against y7_12_49 (wnn_sweep_results.md). V4's port passed code review, but its 22/40 unseen-map result is noisy (AGENT_LOG.md). Fixed queens have no official successor; a post-death keeper is a separate fallback role (queen_succession_and_hunt.md).

Unproven: F1 win improvement (draw-adjusted 19/40), V4 qualifier benefit (9/20 against champion), opening-split gain, post-death modes, wider queen strikes and exact-reach defence (f1_h2h.md; yardstick_vs_champion.md; queen_succession_and_hunt.md). Authentication is a correctness result, not ladder strength. The combined build still needs independent review, CPU validation, mandatory promotion checks and same-time ladder control.

Retraining waits on Kaggle access (task context). The v6 design corrects queen inputs, visible-length skew and late sampling, but remains a specification in the reviewed evidence (dataset_v6_and_bceval.md). Earlier director decision was plane 8 zero in train/play; for these known-map qualifiers the task permits table fill. That is an allowed option, not a measured improvement, and table variants must be checked (AGENT_LOG.md, plane8_decision; plane8_opt1_review.md; loong_blog/DIGEST.md).

Search remains a gated side track: S0 is NO-GO, and the deadline separately disfavors a full rollout build. Python S1 passed its small oracle task, not the larger milestone or an in-bot C++ implementation (search_s0_result.md; engine_sim_status.md).

Top five risks:
1. CPU tail: vB19 reached 76.876M, above the repository's 65M target and below the 100M kill limit; F1 reached 74.716M. New inputs/continuation calls have no measured headroom (cpu_audit.md; f1_h2h.md; AGENTS.md).
2. Evaluation noise and weak yardsticks: champion screens are small; y7_12_49 gains do not establish champion/ladder gains. Historic local optimism is reported, not a universal correction (yardstick_vs_champion.md; nn_plan.md).
3. Queen-death macro gap: current base loses its feeding/protection sink after queen death; false death beliefs could activate the wrong mode (queen_succession_and_hunt.md).
4. Training readiness and parity: Kaggle is pending; public countdowns are missing, tables can vary, and old builder length inputs differ from live visibility (task context; dataset_v6_and_bceval.md; loong_blog/DIGEST.md).
5. Resource/provenance pressure: later reports found roughly 150MB/89MiB disk free; early multi-GB estimates are stale. Replay deletion and differing engines/builds can undermine reproducibility (cpu_audit.md; search_s0_result.md; AGENTS.md).

## Workstreams: findings and open items

### Rule-based foundation and queen modes

F1/F1b: QPOS, EQ and active PPROBE are authenticated; unsigned election input was disabled. Round 2 preserves original observation age and rejects stale gossip; native roundtrip/tampering/expiry tests pass. Its small control comparison does not isolate signing overhead. Sender ID remains plaintext metadata in the reviewed design; keys exposed during review were reportedly rotated and task diffs deleted (AGENT_LOG.md, b19_f1_fixes/b19_f1_round2; sonar_echo_tactics.md; queen_succession_and_hunt.md). Open: metadata-hardening/leak check, unseen-tail runtime coverage, combined-build review and real-champion gate. Security correctness may justify retaining the component, but strength and CPU safety remain gated.

V4: learnGap is called for visible countdowns on unknown maps; counters reset on initialization/dropMap. Critic judged the port correct. Dev result is positive but inconclusive; known-map path is unchanged unless map knowledge is dropped. "No-risk" is not established by a code review or small sample (AGENT_LOG.md, b19_v4_learngap; yardstick_vs_champion.md). Open: confirm qualifier benefit before increasing priority; separately test fresh=pt+1, first-sight estimates, pooled beds and unseen symmetry. These are proposals, not accepted fixes (plane8_opt1_review.md).

Opening: preserve trapped-queen SHED/ESCAPE-SPLIT. Disabling doomedSplit was worse in the tested panel. The dimensional exclusion initially blamed Autarky/Dilemma but also caught Devil; F1 names maps and guards lengths/tails. Devil's observed r0 L4 split count was unchanged, despite a different OPENING-SPLIT path count (AGENT_LOG.md, qdoomed_off/b19_review/b19_f1_fixes). Open: mechanism counts across seeds/maps, unknown-tail scenario, no blanket queen split prohibition.

Succession: official queens stay fixed; death gives score zero, not a replacement queen. Of 142 round-limit top wins, 118 had both queens dead; six living-queen wins beat a longer opposing non-queen (queen_succession_and_hunt.md). Candidate macro: alive/alive feeds true queen; alive/dead hides queen; dead/alive hunts enemy queen and protects a prospective longest; dead/dead restores keeper consolidation. Election/keeper feeding must never replace a live official queen. Open: validate death witnesses/refutation, mode switches and unit floor before flooding death notices. Silence is suspicion, not confirmed death; equal living queen lengths can also invoke lower tiebreaks.

Enemy queen: identify by team plus id<=1, never MY_TEAM alone. Unused helpers were wrong in 168/306 sampled ladder games. Post-opening deaths include 139/350 enemy rams; 76/139 used paid steps. Proposed payable ram reach is R(L)=ceil(L/4)+L-2 without growth, whereas current attack search caps at four steps (queen_succession_and_hunt.md). Open E1/E2/E3: BFS queen strikes, post-death modes, exact-reach queen defence; count kills, queen survival, economy and CPU. A same-round fix is stable only while she remains alive; a nearer ally can kill her before a receiver acts. Exclude our queen from kamikaze hunt, reserve workers and account for death pearls/elimination. EQ homing is partly defective at occupied close-range goals, not globally dead (queen_succession_and_hunt.md).

Sonar extras remain hypotheses: calibrated queen echo alarm, newborn handoff, passive format learning, bed gossip. Echoes cannot directly identify queen/length/distance; inbox jamming is not supported by the mechanics. Historical replay attack was neutral; legality of active forgery remains unverified in that note (sonar_echo_tactics.md). None is a measured qualifier gain.

### CPU safety and resources

vB19 peak 76,876,136 leaves 23,123,864 points below kill; reducing it to 65M requires 11.876M. Schooltime normally disables the net, so adding an assumed network call cannot explain that peak. F1 full-panel peaks are 74,716,414 (A) and 70,520,927 (B), with no failed games (cpu_audit.md; f1_h2h.md). Passing below kill in sampled games does not establish robust headroom.

Structural suspects: linear seen-vector lookup in buildReaches, repeated full-board field evaluation, queued options evaluated after admission guards, per-search rather than cumulative DFS budgets. Exact stamped reach membership is the best behavior-preserving first candidate; cumulative optional-work budgets need fallback and safety-class discipline. Savings are estimates, not profiles. Input/init/output are partly outside elapsedMs accounting (cpu_audit.md).

Replay action statistics are absent, not zero-cost measurements; aggregate logs cannot reveal the worst dragon/round. New profiler commands failed for missing tooling, and disk prevented a build. Preserve stdout CPU columns by candidate side; capture turn telemetry for future profiling (cpu_audit.md). Follow RAM guards, streaming and game parallelism caps in AGENTS.md; check disk afresh before any dataset/panel stage.

Latest detailed v6 spec: 19 spatial planes, six scalars, seven logits including STOP; queen/view-memory input, enemy free-step reach, visible-length parity for planes 12-15, per-dragon continuation states. Plane 18 is reserved zero. Remove late downsampling, filter post-switch top wins, exclude own/98, weight queens x3, cap team concentration and deduplicate. Stream shards and training; ordinary full-RAM loading is infeasible. Public replay bot names cannot support exact latest-submission filtering (dataset_v6_and_bceval.md; search_layer_plan.md).

Scope under deadline: first secure core input/export parity and one affordable retrain; continuation inference is optional until measured CPU headroom exists. Winners used fewer than their free allowance on 86% of drone and 90% of queen moves at L>=5; multi-step moves were about 0.9%, so unused free steps alone are not evidence of a competitive deficit (dataset_v6_and_bceval.md).

Plane-8 history: shipped weights disprove the suspected always-zero channel; zeroing changes argmax in 3.59% of 49,584 local states, first-sight reveal in 0.78%, without measured imitation-accuracy loss. This is not a win-rate test and states are correlated (AGENT_LOG.md, plane8_audit; plane8_sensitivity.md). Earlier director chose zero for exact train/play parity (AGENT_LOG.md, plane8_decision).

Blog author reports variants/unresolved fits; stored tables were checked directly on only one public Autarky game in plane8_opt1_review.md (loong_blog/DIGEST.md). Retain zero as the documented fallback; compare regimes if time permits. Unseen final/sprint need a separate zero or legal-memory regime.

Kaggle access is the current external dependency (task context), not evidence that training is running. Scout snapshot recorded 324 games, 244MB, comprising 306 top wins and 18 own wins; later reports found some inputs deleted (AGENT_LOG.md, scout_v6; search_s0_result.md; engine_sim_status.md). ### Evaluation and yardsticks

Report live/dev separately, preserve map/seed/side pairs, freeze exact source/weights/engine provenance, and count self-driven mechanism changes before reading tiny W/L swings. Do not mix PowerShell and in-WSL panel numbers (AGENTS.md; nn_plan.md). CPU/replay absence is unknown data.

The real-champion 20-game parity screen is not the mandatory gate: AGENTS.md requires >=55% in 40 games versus champion and >=50% in 20 versus V7.5, then decisive ladder panel. V4's FAIL and vB19's PASS use a different 50% threshold and do not prove regression/promotion (yardstick_vs_champion.md; AGENTS.md).

Use same-time near-rating ladder candidate/control comparisons, map by map; local own-lineage matchups cannot certify opponent-dependent tactics. The reported historic approximately 7pp optimism and calc's local-to-ladder shrinkage are warnings, not calibrated present-rule corrections (nn_plan.md). Freeze one control and schedule within 60 unranked games/hour/team (task context; AGENTS.md); do not assume a separate DEV budget without confirmation.

bceval is an old-rule whole-board evaluator, not a legal policy input or proven per-move reward. Its new-rules winner prediction sample is selection-biased; missing public TILEs zero economy features. Local/public scores cannot be compared directly; queen-aware v2 remains an optional secondary-tool proposal (dataset_v6_and_bceval.md).
