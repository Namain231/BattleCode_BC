# New-rules priority list for agent work

Two simultaneous tracks; abandon neither: test every rule idea first on `bots/newrules_rules`, then port every rule change to the hybrid rules + network line (`bots/newrules_q9` / `q9n`; n4, nr1, n4nr). Ranked by likely impact under queen scoring and free sprint. Each item includes the reason for the old decision and a first discriminating test. See [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) for protocol; do not treat old-rule results as current truth.

## Historical signals to revisit, not conclusions

- Candidate swing wins can come from conversion after r100, even when the intended metric did not move. Inspect flipped games and opponent intake/splits.
- Wreck share and feeding volume often follow the winner; mechanism counts alone do not establish causality.
- Old MAPFIX and queen patches changed the rules landscape. Retest only with the new engine and perturbed `maps_ll/` tables.
