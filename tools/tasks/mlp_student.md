# Task mlp_student. Train a small, cheap MLP policy (the calc-team architecture's net) and swap it into our bot. Output: out/mlp_student_report.md (<= 40 lines), candidate dir viper_y96qm.

## Why
The calc team (calc WRITEUP.md in the repo root) runs a small MLP (two hidden layers, 256 and 128) as the policy proposer and then a short search that calls the net many times per turn; they made the net ~8x cheaper per decision (skip zero inputs, SIMD, cache repeated passes). Our conv net (viper_y96qg/policy_nn_v6.hpp, 19 planes + 6 scalars -> 7 logits, ~127k weights) costs ~18-23M points per call, so no search can use it. A cheap student is the prerequisite for any in-bot search and also frees ~15M points of CPU headroom on 64x64 maps.
