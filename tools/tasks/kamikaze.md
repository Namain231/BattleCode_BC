## Rule facts (docs/execution-order.md line 50, docs/death.md, docs/CHANGES_2026-10-01.md)
- A dragon's destination is checked against its own body first, then other dragons: moving into a cell occupied by another dragon's HEAD kills that head first, then the mover (both die, regardless of length). Hitting any other segment kills only the mover.
- Queens are dragon ids 0 and 1 (one per team) and act first each round, so at a scout's turn the enemy queen's head already sits on its post-move cell and stays there until next round: a scout that can reach that exact cell this turn kills her with certainty.
- At round 500 the team with the longer queen wins (dead queen = length 0), then longest dragon, then total length. No immediate loss on queen death. Free steps per turn = ceil(L/4); extra steps cost a tail segment.
- Evidence this matters: enemies already do it to us: our queen died in 503/752 L.10 games (355 head-on), 83/89 Sv5.3 losses (66 head-on); see out/l10_analysis.txt, out/sv53_losses.txt. A small scout trading itself for a 20-segment queen is a very cheap exchange.
