## Directly relevant to our tasks (a)-(e)

### (a) Search layer and engine fidelity

- “Dragons in one game cannot all move together” (`20-an-engine-on-the-gpu.md`). Roll out both teams in sequential ID order, changing the board before each next observation; children can act later that round. Separate ID from slot identity and parallelise trajectories, not turns within one game.
- Start from linked-cell bodies/`Advance`/`Act` in `reference/engine.h`, then implement actual new rules. Old `Step` checks collision before tail removal; split reverses rear links; round starts tick pearls before ordering dragons; action precedes sonar from resulting position/facing, with none after death. Make these explicit new-engine parity cases rather than assume unchanged semantics.
- “A skipped or unsupported game isn't a passing check” (`19-the-machine-inside-the-judge.md`). Compare IDs/full observation bytes per decision and final results under identical replies, stop at first mismatch. Mix invalid/reckless and careful long games; cover portals, split/death, sonar/protocol and free-quota boundaries. Observation parity alone cannot prove hidden-state parity.
- Keep per-dragon recurrent memory/legal knowledge separate from privileged simulator/critic state. Compare tensor and text encodings, rotations/masks/sonar. One-step enemy imitation50–64% (`25-modelling-other-teams.md`) is uncertain; sample alternative enemy replies rather than trust one argmax trajectory.
- Optional work “stops at 75 million” (`26-planning-to-play.md`) is conservative guidance, not our measured budget. Profile actual WASM including output. GPU environment throughput/student MACs do not measure submitted cost. Published planner freezes others outside a two-head duel and omits split/death search, so it does not supply our required rollout layer.

### (b) Sealed sonar and post-death keeper succession

- “at least 14 zero bits” (`26-planning-to-play.md`): round mod 4, chained compact records, SPECK64/128; Exp-Golomb IDs/lengths/ages. Random decrypt passes padding ≈1/16,384 before plausibility. This is foreign-packet filtering, not demonstrated authentication/replay resistance; add freshness/identity/epoch checks to our protocol.
- Ten records:status,sighting,tiles,pearl,path,task,death,order,portal,body. Prioritise recipient-missing facts from post-action ray geometry; avoid rays certain to hit own body/kelp/enemy-only. Carry source/round and expiry; aggregate echoes do not generally resolve ray direction.
- “A dead queen counts as length zero” (`29-slay-the-queen.md`) does not create a replacement official queen. Keep fixed queen0/1 while alive; elect our keeper only after credible death evidence. Old longest-election/20-round role persistence must not replace a live queen. Keeper growth matters when primary queen scores tie, especially both zero.
- Corrected old feeding110/120 (`24-playing-the-ladder.md`) is one local test. Inspect recipient/epoch/death evidence/packet rejection and grade agreement, validity, coverage and spread with knowledge columns. An expired sighting is not confirmed death.

### (c) Enemy queen and kamikaze attacks

- Fixed identity0/1 beats largest length. `29-slay-the-queen.md` provides no tested queen-specific attack algorithm; reinterpret old champion tactics as candidate mechanisms.
- “the new dragon's first move was a ram” (`24-playing-the-ladder.md`). Old foil splits a two-segment child beside an already-moved enemy≥6. Roll out reversed child orientation/newborn order to test reaching the queen before its next turn; enemy tails are potential new heads in queen safety.
- Shadows stayed2–5 cells away for6–8 rounds before ramming; escorts trade cheaply to protect champion value. Track queen-position uncertainty and inspect actual episodes: census shadow false positives show geometry alone is insufficient. Old7/16 late kills/six losses suggest importance, not new-rule win rate.
- Try post26 `strike`, `tailStrike` setup, `portalBlock`, and `standoffSplit` (parent trades/child survives). Recompute ceil(L/4) reach/paid costs and sequential interactions; preserve economy and queen escape. Queen death alone is not elimination, and sacrificing every worker can lose.

### (d) New-rule imitation training

- “the actor and submitted student never can” see critic truth (`27-learning-to-play.md`). Reconstruct legal remembered map/sonar/lifecycle; omitted countdowns need checked restoration or explicit unknowns, never true hidden board in actor crops.
- Reuse forward-up crop/mirror augmentation with transformed labels/sonar, recurrent life chunks/burn-in, compressed mmap shards and split/sprint/sacrifice targets. Our recommendation: split validation by game/submission/time, tag engine hashes, separate old/new-rule samples; article opponent fits only claim held-out-game evaluation.
-44M teacher/four-H100 private unfinished recipe is beyond our machine. Normalised recurrent state/structured actions/quantisation gates may transfer;50–64% move fits do not copy a team's strategy. Demonstrations support DAPG/recurrent warm starts, not arbitrary-state DAgger with unavailable enemy programs.
- “the longer queen wins” (`29-slay-the-queen.md`) requires queen-aware objectives/masks; longest-only shaping is obsolete as primary target. Keep longest/total for ties and update affordable steps. Allow useful intentional deaths if legal; measure ordinary-game CPU after inspection parity.
- Pin every input per `14-three-lines.md`, including engine/runtime/compiler/schema/weights/headers. Compare integer Python/C++/WASM on multi-turn sequences, not one forward vector; atomic resumable checkpoints, immutable evaluated snapshots.

### (e) Online bed-gap learning

- “An occupied tile still makes its attempt and consumes that draw” (`10-pearls-from-the-seed.md`). Learn resets/attempts rather than only appearances; separate corpse drops; test c−1 initial versus r+c reset indexing and symmetry-group RNG chronology.
- Post26 renewal timing distribution/two-state presence HMM forecasts64 rounds. Update from live countdown resets; retain uncertainty over missed attempts/eating and evaluate pearl chance at arrival time. Known geometry does not identify private gap variants.
