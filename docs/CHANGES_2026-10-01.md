# Rule and docs changes, 2026-10-01

The organisers changed the rules after the sprint tournament. The pages in this folder were re-fetched from
https://game.battlecode.au/docs on 2026-10-01 and replace the earlier copies; every page names its source URL
under the title. The new client is `unswbc` 1.2.3 (released 2026-10-01). It carries the new engine, so local
matches on 1.2.2 or older play the old rules. Upgrade with `pip install -U unswbc`.

## Game rules

| Page | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `structure.md` (scoring) | After round 500: longest living dragon, then total length, then draw. | After round 500: (1) **longer queen**, where a dead queen counts as length 0; (2) longest living dragon; (3) total length; (4) draw. The queens are dragon id 0 and dragon id 1. |
| `overview.md` | Goal was the longest dragon. | "your queen, the lowest-id robot of your team, (and its pearls) will be extracted back to the surface ... grow the longest queen dragon!" |
| `movement.md` (sprinting) | x steps cost x − 1 segments (first step free). | "A dragon of length L takes its first ceil(L / 4) steps of a turn free, with L fixed when the move starts." x steps cost max(0, x − ceil(L/4)) segments: 5 steps cost a length-4 dragon 4 segments, a length-12 dragon 2. The organisers say the constant 4 may be rebalanced. |
| `execution-order.md` | "If this is not the first step, check that the dragon can pay for it." | The check, and the extra tail segment, apply only after the first ceil(L/4) steps. |
| `map-files.md` | Any dragon order. | "Teams must alternate from line to line, so dragons 0 and 1, the queens, are on different teams." Also new: a tile with `maxGap` above 0 needs `minGap` of at least 1. |

Unchanged: sonar, vision, pearls, splitting, death, protocol (still 3.0.0).

What follows from the unchanged pages:
- A bot knows its own id (`ID` in the init block, `get_id()`), so each dragon knows whether it is the queen.
- Every `DRAGON_BODIES` line in vision is `team id x y facing isHead`. So the enemy queen (id 0 or 1, on the other team) is recognisable whenever any of its segments is in view.
- On a split the child takes the next unused id and the parent keeps its own. A queen that splits stays the queen: the head end, which keeps moving.
- Sonar carries no sender id. Locating the enemy queen still takes vision.

## Ratings and matchmaking

| Page | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `elo.md` | One Elo per team; a new build moved faster for its first 10 ranked battles. | Rewritten. Each submission has its own rating plus an offset on every map, kept as a mean and an uncertainty (a Kalman-filter update per game). Team rating = active submission's overall rating + its mean offset over the ranked pool. A new submission starts from the previous ranked one with ±75 extra uncertainty. Re-uploading identical code resumes that code's rating. Moving first is worth +20. Tiers: Swordfish 1500–1899, Shark 1900–2299, and so on. |
| `game-format.md` | Hourly autoscrims; no second battle against a team while one is pending. | Adds continuous autoscrims ("at times ... the sidebar says so"): a fixed rate, equal numbers per team (fewest in the last 24 h goes next), opponents within 50 places weighted towards close games and teams not met recently, one at a time. The hourly text is still on the page too. DEV-team battles have their own 60 games/hour. The one-pending-battle-per-opponent limit is gone. |
| `api.md` | | Same endpoints. DEV battles get a separate 60 games/hour. |

The organisers' slides add that all past submissions were marked inactive, and that continuous autoscrims run at 3 per hour per team for now.

## Our files this affects

- `game-mechanics-summary.md`: updated for the queen rule and the sprint cost.
- `maps_rm_dev/rdense_*.map`, `maps_rm_test/rdense_*.map`: these listed all of team A's dragons first. Their `DRAGON` lines are now interleaved A, B, A, B, and the new engine runs them. Maps from `tools/gen_random_maps.py` (`maps_gen/`, `maps_dense*/`) probably have the same problem; check them, and fix the generator to alternate teams.
- Tools built on the old ladder no longer fit: the hourly-autoscrim timing in `elo_bot.py` and `strong_panel.py`, and the K / first-10-battles reasoning in AGENTS.md.
- Bot code: any sprint-cost arithmetic that assumes x − 1 (reach L − 1, cost checks) under-uses the free steps. It stays safe, because it over-pays in its own estimate, never in the engine. Any "grow whichever dragon is longest" queen logic must now grow dragon 0 or 1 specifically.

## Local judge

The zig judge (`the-loong-game/harness/zig_judge`) takes the winner from the engine (`src/engine.zig:184`) and only labels the reason, so it follows whatever engine it is given. A queen win is still labelled "on length". `tools/panel_zig.py` uses the installed `unswbc`'s `unswbc_engine.wasm` (or `UNSWBC_ENGINE`), so upgrade to 1.2.3 before running panels.
